Our Legacy

Tradition served with purity, care, and quiet luxury.

Shree Bankey Bihari Misthan Bhandar was built on the simple promise that every sweet should taste like celebration and every snack should feel freshly made at home. Our recipes honor Indian mithai traditions while our kitchen follows modern hygiene, careful sourcing, and consistent quality.

From hand-finished kaju katli and syrup-rich gulab jamun to crisp kachori and festive gift boxes, each batch is prepared with patience, clean ingredients, and the unmistakable warmth of Indian hospitality.
